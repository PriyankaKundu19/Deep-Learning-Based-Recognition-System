Compounds found in medicinal plants have therapeutic qualities that are very
useful in treating various diseases and preserving good health. For that reason,
throughout history medicinal plants have been utilized by many cultures to treat
a wide range of illnesses and enhance general health. Distinguishing between toxic
and medicinal plants is critical due to the inherent dangers posed by some botanical
species. However, recognizing plants with therapeutic properties is challenging for
the general people, as it requires specific knowledge and discernment. In this pa
per, we have collected a dataset containing 2884 images of 15 medicinal plants (rich
in therapeutic quality) and developed an automatic deep-learning-based recogni
tion system, MediPlantNet. Our evaluation on different deep learning models
shows that the EfficientNet B5 achieves the highest validation accuracy of 99%.
